Why BrightSmile

Dentistry that treats you like a person, not a patient number.

We started BrightSmile in 2005 with one simple idea: dental visits shouldn't be something you dread. Today our six-dentist team sees patients of every age, in a clinic built around comfort — natural light, quiet treatment rooms, and a front desk that actually explains your insurance instead of just handing you a bill.

  • Digital X-rays with 80% less radiation than film
  • Weeknight and Saturday appointment hours
  • In-house financing plans, no interest for 12 months
  • A dedicated pediatric room for nervous first-timers
